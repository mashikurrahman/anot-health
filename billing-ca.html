Where provincial billing usually leaks revenue

Most Canadian clinics are not losing money on rejected claims. They are losing it on claims that were never submitted, never resubmitted, or never reconciled.

Leak 1

Rejections that never get resubmitted

A provincial rejection is not a lost claim unless nobody works it. We track every rejection to resolution instead of letting the remittance file absorb it silently.

Leak 2

Premiums nobody claimed

After-hours, special visit, chronic disease management, and age-based premiums are eligible far more often than they are billed — particularly in busy primary care.

Leak 3

Reconciliation that stops at the deposit

Matching the remittance advice line by line against what was submitted is where underpayments surface. Stopping at the bank deposit hides them.
