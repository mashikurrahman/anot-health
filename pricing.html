Questions teams ask about pricing and scope

These are the questions that usually matter most when deciding whether to start with a public plan or move directly into a tailored operational subscription.

Is pricing per provider or per practice?+

The public structure is designed around monthly plan levels rather than a single flat rate. Foundation starts at $1,000, Professional starts at $1,500, and Enterprise is scoped separately when the workflow requires it.

What determines whether we stay in Foundation or move to Professional or Enterprise?+

The main drivers are dictation complexity, specialty nuance, speaker variability, note density, and visit length. As those increase, the workflow may require a stronger review model and move into a higher plan.

What is included in the Foundation plan?+

The Foundation plan starts at $1,000/month and is built for clear, consistent dictation with AI-first note generation and light human QA for final cleanup. It is intentionally the lightest review model in the public pricing structure.

When does Enterprise pricing apply?+

Enterprise pricing applies when the documentation profile is substantially more complex or when your team needs broader workflow support, implementation planning, or cross-functional operational scope beyond the standard public plans.
