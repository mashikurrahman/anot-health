Questions teams ask about pricing and scope

If something here does not match your situation, a walkthrough will answer it faster than a pricing page can.

Is pricing per provider or per practice?+

Per provider, per month. A three-physician clinic on Verified Scribe is billed for three providers. Group and multi-site practices usually move to Custom, where the rate is scoped against total volume rather than a per-seat count.

What actually changes between $99 and $599?+

People. The capture, the AI drafting and the EHR delivery are the same on both plans. On AI Scribe the note reaches you as a draft and you are the reviewer. On Verified Scribe a certified scribe reads every note and a QPS auditor checks the clinical detail first, so what reaches you is ready to sign. You are paying for trained human review, which is a labour cost rather than a software one.

Can I start on AI Scribe and upgrade later?+

Yes, and most practices do. Your templates, specialty settings and EHR connection carry over, so upgrading adds the review layer without rebuilding anything. You can move at any month boundary, in either direction.

What happens past 400 reviewed notes in a month?+

Nothing stops. We flag it and talk about whether your volume is better served by Custom pricing, which is scoped around actual throughput. Going slightly over in a busy month is not billed as an overage surprise.

Is my data less protected on the cheaper plan?+

No. HIPAA-compliant handling, the BAA, AES-256 encryption, TLS 1.3 and our commitment never to train public models on your patient data apply identically on every plan. Security is not a paid upgrade.

When does pricing become Custom?+

When you need more than documentation — revenue cycle, coding or payroll on the same team — or when volume, multi-site coverage, integration work or procurement requirements make a per-seat rate the wrong shape.
