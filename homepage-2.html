Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our clinical operations, human QA, and integration.

How does the Human-in-the-Loop QA layer work?
After our high-speed speech AI generates a preliminary SOAP note draft, it is immediately routed to a certified medical scribe and senior QPS reviewer. They cross-verify medication dosages, patient history references, and clinical terminology against your practice's specific templates, ensuring 100% verified clinical accuracy before it reaches your EHR.
Does Anot Health integrate directly with my EHR system?
Yes. We integrate directly with Epic, Oracle Cerner, athenahealth, eClinicalWorks, NextGen, Meditech, and all other major systems via secure FHIR APIs, HL7 feeds, or certified browser bridge connectors. You do not need to copy and paste notes manually.
Is patient audio and clinical data HIPAA compliant?
Yes, 100%. We execute standard Business Associate Agreements (BAAs). All audio and data streams are encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit within dedicated HIPAA-compliant AWS infrastructure. Audio recordings are never retained long-term or used for model training.
How fast can our practice get started?
Practices can be fully live in under 7 business days. Our clinical onboarding team imports your favorite note templates, establishes your EHR connection, and pairs you with your dedicated scribe and QPS auditor with zero workflow disruption.
What medical specialties do you support?
We support over 50 distinct medical specialties, including Family Medicine, Cardiology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, Neurology, Dermatology, Urgent Care, Behavioral Health, and Surgical subspecialties.
