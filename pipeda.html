1. Provincial Privacy Frameworks Covered

Anot Health adheres to all relevant Canadian health information privacy acts:

  • Federal PIPEDA: Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act compliance across all interprovincial workflows.
  • Ontario PHIPA: Personal Health Information Protection Act requirements for Health Information Custodians (HICs) and service agents.
  • Alberta HIA: Health Information Act compliance for Alberta custodians, clinics, and Primary Care Networks (PCNs).
  • British Columbia PIPA: Personal Information Protection Act standards for private clinics and health organizations.
  • Quebec Law 25: Modernized protection of personal information and stringent privacy governance.

2. Information Manager Agreements (IMA)

Before launching with any Canadian practice, Anot Health executes an Information Manager Agreement (IMA) or Business Associate / Data Processing Agreement (DPA) tailored to your province. This formal contract establishes our legal duties as an Information Manager under provincial statutes, ensuring patient data confidentiality and security accountability.

3. Strict In-Country Data Residency

Unlike US-centric AI scribes that route patient audio and chart transcripts to US East/West servers, Anot Health operates dedicated instances inside AWS Canada Central. Patient conversations and clinical records are processed and stored strictly within Canadian territory, fully honoring Canadian public healthcare data residency mandates.

4. Zero AI Training on Patient Health Information (PHI)

Your patients' conversations and clinical charts are never used to train public language models or commercial AI datasets. Our speech engine operates within isolated, Canadian-hosted environments. Once notes are verified and successfully signed into your Canadian EMR (Accuro, Telus PS Suite, Med Access, OSCAR), encounter audio is purged according to your practice's data retention policy.

5. Two-Stage Human QA Firewall

Pure AI transcription models frequently introduce dangerous hallucinations in dosage units, lateralities, and diagnoses. Anot Health protects Canadian clinicians with a certified human QA review layer: a trained medical scribe drafts the note, and a Quality, Performance & Safety (QPS) auditor validates every clinical detail before chart delivery.

Common diligence questions from Canadian physicians and clinic administrators.

Is patient data stored in Canada?+

Yes, 100%. All processing, speech transcription, database storage, and backup systems operate strictly within AWS Canada Central (Montreal/Calgary). No data leaves Canada at any point in the workflow.

Does Anot Health sign an Information Manager Agreement (IMA)?+

Yes. We execute an Information Manager Agreement (IMA) compliant with provincial legislation (e.g. Ontario PHIPA, Alberta HIA, BC PIPA) with every clinic and provider prior to go-live.

Are audio recordings used to train public AI models?+

Never. Patient audio and clinical text are processed inside private Canadian instances and are strictly segregated. Audio is never used to train third-party public models.

Our scribes and platform deliver verified notes directly into your favorite EMR templates inside Accuro, Telus PS Suite, Med Access, or OSCAR Pro. Physicians review and sign with the fastest turnaround time.
