Frequently Asked Canadian Coding Questions

Common questions from Canadian clinical directors and group practices.

How do you handle provincial diagnostic variations?+

Each province maintains unique diagnostic coding rules (for example, OHIP uses a simplified 3-digit diagnostic table, while BC MSP and AHCIP use ICD-9-based systems). Our Canadian specialists code specifically for your province's ministry requirements.

Do you code in ICD-10-CA?+

Only where it belongs. ICD-10-CA and CCI are CIHI's facility standards, used when a hospital or day surgery unit abstracts to DAD or NACRS. Physician claims to a provincial plan are not coded that way — your office billing goes out against the plan's own diagnostic table (OHIP, MSP, AHCIP or your provincial equivalent) and its fee schedule. We keep the two workflows separate so neither one contaminates the other.

Do you support hospital outpatient and Day Surgery coding?+

Yes. We support CIHI DAD (Discharge Abstract Database) and NACRS (National Ambulatory Care Reporting System) abstraction standards for surgical and hospital-affiliated clinics.
