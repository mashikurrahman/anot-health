Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Canadian operations, privacy compliance, and EMR integration.

How does Anot Health comply with Canadian privacy laws (PIPEDA & PHIPA)?
All patient data is stored and processed strictly within Canadian borders on dedicated AWS Canada Central infrastructure. We comply fully with federal PIPEDA, Ontario's PHIPA, Alberta's Health Information Act (HIA), and British Columbia's PIPA. Data is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Audio recordings are never retained long-term or used for public AI training.
Which Canadian EMRs do you integrate with?
We integrate directly with QHR Accuro EMR, Telus Health (PS Suite, Med Access, Wolf), OSCAR Pro (WELL Health), and hospital-based Epic Canada systems. Notes are placed into your chart ready for your sign-off without manual copy-pasting.
Do you support provincial medical billing (OHIP, MSP, AHCIP)?
Yes. Our billing specialists handle provincial fee-for-service claims, shadow billing for rostered/capitated physicians (FHO/FHN in Ontario), diagnostic code cross-referencing, and error reconciliation to ensure timely payment.
What currency are your plans billed in?
All plans for Canadian clinics are priced and billed in Canadian Dollars ($ CAD) with transparent monthly invoicing and applicable GST/HST handling.
How fast can our Canadian practice get started?
Onboarding takes less than 7 business days. Our clinical team sets up your EMR connection, imports your preferred note templates, and pairs your practice with certified medical scribes and QPS auditors.
