Frequently Asked Canadian Pricing Questions

Clear answers on billing cadence, currency, and contract flexibility for Canadian medical practices.

Is pricing billed in Canadian Dollars ($ CAD)?+

Yes. All subscriptions for Canadian healthcare practices are billed in Canadian Dollars ($ CAD). You will never be subject to USD exchange rate swings or international credit card transaction fees.

Are there long-term locked contracts?+

No. We operate on month-to-month clinical service agreements. You can adjust provider seats or service tiers as clinic staffing and visit volumes evolve.

Can multiple physicians in a Family Health Team share a plan?+

We offer customized group discounting for practices with 3 or more physicians, including dedicated scribe calibration for each doctor's preferred note layout.

Can we bundle provincial billing with scribing?+

Yes. Many clinics bundle our clinical scribing with provincial billing (OHIP, MSP, AHCIP) for a complete hands-off operational workflow that optimizes revenue and stops billing leaks.
