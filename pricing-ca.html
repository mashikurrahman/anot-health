Frequently Asked Canadian Pricing Questions

If something here does not match your situation, a walkthrough will answer it faster than a pricing page can.

Is pricing billed in Canadian Dollars ($ CAD)?+

Yes. Every plan is billed in Canadian dollars. You will never be exposed to USD exchange rate swings or international credit card transaction fees.

What actually changes between $99 and $599?+

People. The capture, the AI drafting and the EMR delivery are the same on both plans. On AI Scribe the note reaches you as a draft and you are the reviewer. On Verified Scribe a certified scribe reads every note and a QPS auditor checks the clinical detail first, so what reaches you is ready to sign. You are paying for trained human review, which is a labour cost rather than a software one.

Can multiple physicians in a Family Health Team share a plan?+

Pricing is per provider, so each physician using the service is billed. Family Health Teams and multi-site groups usually move to Custom, where the rate is scoped against total volume rather than a per-seat count.

Can we bundle provincial billing with scribing?+

Yes. Provincial claim submission and Remittance Advice reconciliation are available as an add-on to Verified Scribe, and are included in scope on Custom plans alongside coding and payroll.

Is my data less protected on the cheaper plan?+

No. In-country residency in AWS Canada Central, PIPEDA and provincial privacy alignment, the Information Manager Agreement, AES-256 encryption and our commitment never to train public models on your patient data apply identically on every plan. Privacy is not a paid upgrade.

Are there long-term locked contracts?+

No. Plans are month to month, and you can move between tiers at any month boundary in either direction. Your EMR templates and specialty settings carry over when you do.
